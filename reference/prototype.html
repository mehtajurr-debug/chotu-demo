Nothing here listens or calls an AI. The chips stand in for Baba's voice, and Chotu's lines appear as captions. In the real build, a short voice clip goes to Gemini to find the meaning, and Chotu speaks with a generated voice. On the elder phone, touch Chotu's face when it is idle to start a free conversation. The caregiver phone is tappable: open Baba's card, the alert, the summary, and Add medicine.
